Rung 1 — the weak-tie rep (daily)

One 30-second exchange with a peripheral person already in your orbit — barista, gym regular, dog-park person. Greet, one real question or comment, done.
Tag: diary studies — early evidence; days with more acquaintance interaction associated with more happiness and belonging. Same-day well-being and forecast reps, not friendship.

Rung 2 — the lapsed-tie text (one per week, minimum)

Text one person you've drifted from. Scripts:

  1. "Was just thinking about that trip to __. How ARE you?"
  2. "Saw __ and thought of you immediately."
  3. "Been way too long. Coffee next week — Tuesday or Thursday?"

Graded on sent, never on reply.
Tag: 13 preregistered experiments — appreciation for reach-outs is underestimated, MOST for socially distant ties; one of the experiments independently replicated at n = 742, and held.

Rung 3 — the repeat-hours move (on any warm response)

Convert warmth into a recurring leisure slot: day, time, cadence, named activity. "Same time next Tuesday?"

Hours-math box: roughly 50 hours ≈ casual friend · roughly 90 more ≈ friend · 200-plus ≈ close friend — approximate, self-reported estimates. Work hours count less; leisure hours drive closeness. A 2-hour weekly slot crosses the roughly-50-hour line in about six months. "Let's hang out sometime" crosses nothing.
Tag: Hall 2019, Journal of Social and Personal Relationships — hours predicted closeness; leisure hours specifically.

Rung 4 — the initiator's turn (when Rung 3 holds)

Own a standing invite — weekly walk, monthly dinner — and hold it open. You send the reminder; you show up even when it's two of you.
Tag: randomized commuter experiments — initiating felt better than forecast, and being approached felt just as good as initiating. The host is not imposing.

Rules, printed where you'll see them

  • One Rung 2 text per week, minimum.
  • Predicted-vs-actual log, every rep: forecast 1–10 before, actual after.
  • No skipping rungs upward — Rung 3 without Rung 2 is why "join a club" failed.
  • A silent week costs one text, not the method.

Everything above is an average tendency — most people, most of the time. The ladder never requires it to be true of any one person. Graded on sent.
